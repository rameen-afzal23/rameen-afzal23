## 💫 About Me

<div align="center">

**Agentic AI Engineer in Training&nbsp; · &nbsp;On the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Path&nbsp; · &nbsp;Founder @TheRameen**

*Methodology: Learn with intention, build with trust.*

</div>

Assalam o Alaikum! I'm Rameen Afzal — and I believe the best plans are the ones Allah makes for us.

I came from a Commerce background, not Computer Science. AI Architecture wasn't planned — it was chosen for me. Since January 2025, I've been on a dedicated mission to become a Certified Agentic AI Architect at Panaversity (PIAIC), training on the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) path toward becoming an Agentic AI Engineer.

**🎯 What I'm training in:**
- 🤖 Driving general agents (**Claude Code**, **OpenCode**) to decompose real problems and execute specs, not just prompts
- 🔁 Designing loops an agent can run on its own — act, check, decide, repeat
- 🐍 **Python** as my core language, written to work alongside agents
- 👷 Building **Digital FTEs** — AI workers with memory, skills, and a defined job, not a chatbot with a prompt
- 🧩 Composing AI workers into a coordinated workforce with identity and delegated authority
- 🚀 Shipping agentic systems to production and defending the architecture behind them

> 💡 An agent is only as good as the harness around it — the model is the brain, discipline makes it trustworthy: spec first, agent executes, human validates.

- 🏥 Put that training into practice by building **Sehat AI**, Pakistan's AI Health Assistant, and shipping it live on Google Cloud Run
- 📚 Still early in the journey — every project here is a real attempt at learning by building, not a finished expert showcase
- 🎬 Founder [@TheRameen](https://www.youtube.com/@TheRameenOfficial) — a space for the unfiltered truth behind every success story
- 📍 Karachi, Pakistan

🌱 **Long-term goal:** Build reusable AI capabilities that solve real business problems — permanently, at scale.
